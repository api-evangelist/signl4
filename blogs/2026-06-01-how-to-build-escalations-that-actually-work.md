Modern IT operations teams struggle with incident response not because monitoring fails, but because the right person does not respond quickly enough. SIGNL4 advocates for automated escalation workflows across three tiers: ensuring alerts cannot be ignored through multi-channel notifications, automatically escalating unacknowledged alerts between defined responder tiers, and extending escalation across teams during major incidents to prevent operational stalls. The guide covers configuring duty schedules, defining escalation tiers, and managing alert timing across teams.
